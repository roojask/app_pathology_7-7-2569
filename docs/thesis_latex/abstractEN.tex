Gross examination in surgical pathology requires data entry while wearing gloves contaminated with specimens and formalin, and the laboratory adds fume hood noise, rapid dictation and mid-sentence self-correction, which degrade conventional speech-to-text engines. This project presents PathoWhisper, an offline system for breast cancer gross examination that combines afftdn noise reduction, a Faster-Whisper Small INT8 engine with a CAP vocabulary prompt, and a deterministic extractor that fills a 15-field form and generates a PDF report.

On a benchmark of 1,000 synthetic-speech cases in 10 categories (including 100 cases with recorded fume hood noise overlaid), the case-averaged Word Error Rate (WER) fell from 36.27\% for a Whisper Small baseline to 29.05\% (95\% CI of the reduction: 6.26--8.14 points), and from 7.25\% to 2.78\% after normalizing dimension formatting. Under fume hood noise ($-10$ dB SNR), WER fell from 44.88\% to 31.94\%, and mean processing time fell from 20.04 s to 11.50 s per case (1.74$\times$ faster). For information extraction, Macro-F1 over the 11 fields with positive reference values rose slightly from 96.26\% to 96.97\%, largely owing to the breast-side field. The proportion of cases with at least one wrong critical field fell from 27.5\% to 19.6\% (paired difference 7.9 points, 95\% CI 5.3--10.6). However, 10.0\% of cases fail because of the extractor itself even on error-free reference text, the number of wrong values filled in rose from 146 to 164, specimen and mass dimensions remained at an F1 of about 88\%, and a false infiltrative-mass finding was extracted in 12 of 100 benign cases. The extractor was refined after inspecting errors on the same test set, the component ablation covers only 5 cases, and the four fields with no positive reference values were not tested for detection ability. The results therefore require confirmation on independent data and real speech. The system is a data-entry aid, and users must review its output before confirmation.
