Gross examination in surgical pathology requires data entry while wearing gloves contaminated with specimens and formalin, and the laboratory adds fume hood noise, rapid dictation and mid-sentence self-correction, which degrade conventional speech-to-text engines. This project presents PathoWhisper, an offline system for breast cancer gross examination that combines afftdn noise reduction, a Faster-Whisper Small INT8 engine with a CAP vocabulary prompt, and a deterministic extractor that fills a 15-field form and generates a PDF report.

On a synthetic-speech benchmark of 1,000 cases in 10 categories (the 100 fume hood cases are overlaid with recorded fan noise), the case-averaged Word Error Rate (WER) decreased from 36.27\% to 29.05\% compared with a Whisper Small baseline (95\% CI of the reduction: 6.28--8.16 points), and from 7.25\% to 2.78\% after normalizing dimension formatting. Under fume hood noise ($-10$ dB SNR), WER decreased from 44.88\% to 31.94\%. Mean processing time decreased from 20.04 s to 11.50 s per case (1.74$\times$ faster). Field extraction was evaluated on all 15 fields: \nSpoken{} fields are mentioned in the test set, and their macro-averaged F1 changed from \MacroBase\% to \MacroPW\%, while \nNeg{} fields are never mentioned and serve as negative controls. The F1 difference between the two systems is much smaller than the WER difference, and the weakest field is the 3D tumor size (recall \RPWMassDims\%). Unit tests of the self-correction and negation rules passed \unitPass{} of \unitTotal{} cases. The system is a data-entry aid, and users must review its output before confirmation.
