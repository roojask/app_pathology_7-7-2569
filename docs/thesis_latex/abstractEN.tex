This project presents \textbf{PathoWhisper}, an offline artificial intelligence system for speech recognition and clinical data extraction designed for breast cancer surgical pathology gross examinations on personal computers. The system automatically transcribes spoken pathology dictations and populates a standardized 15-field College of American Pathologists (CAP) protocol form, eliminating chemical-glove operating barriers, minimizing manual documentation errors, and ensuring strict patient privacy in compliance with PDPA and HIPAA regulations. The developed architecture integrates four key components: (1) an automated noise-screening mechanism (\textbf{Blind SNR Gate}); (2) \textbf{phonetic-contrastive prompt conditioning} ($P_4$); (3) \textbf{Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA)} fine-tuning of the \textbf{Whisper Small} model compressed to 8-bit integer quantization (\textbf{INT8}) for CPU execution; and (4) a 15-field CAP form extractor integrated with a \textbf{Multi-Signal Safety Flagging} mechanism. Evaluated on 1,000 simulated clinical cases under a zero-phrase-leakage split, the system correctly classified acoustic noise conditions at 98.67\% and reduced the case-level critical error rate ($\text{CER}_{\text{case}}$) on test-seen phrases to 5.33\% (from 12.67\% in the baseline) with a mean macro F1-score of 99.13\% across all 15 fields. For test-unseen speakers and vocabulary, $\text{CER}_{\text{case}}$ was reduced to 11.33\% (from 24.00\%), while maintaining rapid processing latencies of 6.07--6.57 seconds per case. Furthermore, the safety flagging mechanism effectively constrained unflagged silent critical errors. PathoWhisper provides an accurate, fast, noise-robust, and clinically safe offline assistant for preliminary pathology report generation.
