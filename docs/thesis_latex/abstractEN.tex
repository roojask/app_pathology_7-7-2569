Gross examination in surgical pathology is a crucial procedure where pathologists must record clinical data while wearing gloves contaminated with specimens and formalin, under an environment characterized by fume hood fan noise, rapid dictation, and mid-sentence self-corrections, which pose significant challenges to conventional speech-to-text systems. This project presents PathoWhisper, an offline speech recognition and information extraction system for breast cancer gross examination operating locally on personal computers. The system integrates spectral noise reduction (afftdn), an INT8-quantized Faster-Whisper Small model conditioned on a standard College of American Pathologists (CAP) vocabulary prompt, and a deterministic information extractor to populate a standardized 15-field form and automatically generate PDF reports.

Evaluation on a benchmark of 1,000 synthetic audio cases across 10 clinical testing categories (including 100 cases with fume hood noise at $-10$ dB SNR) demonstrated that PathoWhisper reduced the average Word Error Rate (WER) from 36.27\% in the baseline model (Whisper Small) to 29.05\%, and further to 2.78\% after normalizing dimension formatting under Criterion B. Under fume hood noise, the WER decreased from 44.88\% to 31.94\%, and the average processing time decreased from 20.04 seconds to 11.50 seconds per case (1.74$\times$ faster). In terms of information extraction, the Macro-$F_1$ across 11 primary fields increased from 97.79\% to 98.61\%, and the proportion of cases with at least one critical field error ($\mathrm{CER}_{\mathrm{case}}$) decreased from 18.80\% to 11.10\%. Nonetheless, limitations remain in extracting mass dimensions and deep margin distances ($F_1$ scores of 95.9\% and 96.3\%, respectively), alongside false-positive detections of infiltrative mass in 12 out of 100 benign cases. The extractor was refined after inspecting errors on the same test set, so the extraction figures are upper bounds that require confirmation on independent data and real speech. Overall, PathoWhisper was developed as an offline preliminary data-entry assistive system to alleviate physical typing burdens, while examining pathologists retain the essential responsibility of verifying accuracy and confirming the final report.
